Why these invoices are worth keeping

Issued means issued

An issued invoice is immutable. Fixing a mistake is a void with a reason and a fresh invoice — the number stays burned, the history stays honest. Exactly what your accountant wishes you did.

Your details, frozen in time

The seller block — your name, address, payment instructions — freezes onto each invoice at issue. Change banks next year; last year's invoices still say what they said.

An agent that can't make things up

It never invents an amount, a rate, or a date — it asks you. And everything it does lands in an audit trail you can read, refusals included.

Who owes what, honestly

Payments are recorded as they arrive — unapplied money is a real, visible state, never silently matched. Outstanding shows the truth, oldest first.
