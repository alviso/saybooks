The terms for using Saybooks

Effective 2026-11-02. Saybooks is operated by Crankk Inc ("we"). It is built and run by Peter Varga, its founder, who wrote these terms in plain words. They cover the hosted service at saybooks.io, the connector your AI assistant uses, and the Saybooks apps for Mac and iPhone. Questions go to hello@saybooks.io.

The short version

  • Saybooks is free to use. If a paid plan ever exists, it will be announced, and prices change with twelve months' notice.
  • Your books are yours. You can export everything or delete it at any time.
  • Saybooks records. It never emails your customers, never charges a card, never moves money.
  • It is not financial, tax or legal advice, and it is provided as it is.
  • Don't use it to break the law, to reach books that aren't yours, or to strain the service for everyone else.

Your account and your books

You sign in to saybooks.io with a Google account. You are responsible for what goes into the spaces you own and for the people you invite into them. Anonymous demo sandboxes need no account and are deleted after 24 hours.

What you put in your books stays yours. You give us permission to store and process it only to run the service for you: to keep it and show it to you and the people and agents you let in. We do not sell it, share it, advertise with it, or train anything on it. The privacy page lists exactly what is stored and for how long.

Agents and keys

When you connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another agent, it acts as your delegate: under your name, with the role you gave its key, and every act it takes lands in your audit log with the agent named. You decide which agents to connect and what they may do, and you are responsible for what they do with the access you gave them. A key or a document link works for whoever holds it, so treat it like a password and revoke it when you no longer want it to work.

The rules in your books hold for an agent exactly as they hold for you. Some acts, such as approving a bill for payment, are refused to agents whatever their role. Your agreement with your agent's provider governs what that provider does with what the agent sees.

What Saybooks is not

  • Not a bank or a payment service. It holds no money and moves none. A payment is recorded after it happened.
  • Not advice. Figures, summaries and year totals are worked out from what you and your agent put in. A statement is taken only when its rows add up to the balances printed on it, but Saybooks cannot know whether the statement itself is right. Check the figures that matter, especially before you file taxes, and ask a professional when the decision is a professional one.
  • Not a place for secrets. Don't store passwords, full card numbers or government identity numbers in your books. Saybooks keeps only the last four digits of an account, and a full card number handed to it is cut to its last four before anything is stored.

Fair use

Don't use Saybooks to do anything unlawful, to store data you have no right to hold, to try to reach someone else's books, to probe or attack the service, or to send it so many requests that it slows down for others. The service limits how fast any one address can call it, so it stays fair for everyone.

The apps run on your own devices and keep your books there. Nothing passes through a server of ours unless you point them at books on saybooks.io, and then these terms apply to those books as to any others. The model the Mac app runs and the search it can use are provided by others, under their own terms.

Availability and changes

saybooks.io is run by one person. It is meant to be up all the time, but there is no guaranteed uptime, and features may change, move or go away as the product grows. If the hosted service is ever shut down, it will be announced on the site and by email to everyone with a space, at least 60 days ahead, so there is time to export. Export works any day, and the export file can be brought into Saybooks for Mac.

Ending

You can stop at any time: delete your spaces, and email us if you want your account removed as well. If someone breaks these terms, we may suspend their access, and where we can we will say why and give them the chance to export first.
