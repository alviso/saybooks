What the rules catch

The mistakes a busy search makes

Warnings come to you to weigh. Nothing is quietly resolved behind your back.

The double-submission guard

Agencies retitle the same job constantly. The guard matches on the req id, the link, the employer, and the description itself, reordered templates included.

Never an invented fact

An application date that was never recorded stays unknown, and the agent is refused if it tries to guess. When the true date turns up, a correction records it with its source.

Ghosted is a status, not a deletion

Silence is an outcome, and which companies go quiet is worth knowing. Closed pursuits stay on the books, one click away.

One open next action

Each pursuit has one thing to do next, with a due date. The Due page is your morning list; plans you replaced are kept, not erased.
