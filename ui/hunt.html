What the rules catch

The double-submission guard

Agencies retitle the same req constantly. The guard matches on req id, URL, employer — and the job description body itself, reordered templates included. Warnings are surfaced for you to weigh, never silently resolved.

Never an invented fact

An application date that was never recorded stays "unknown" — the agent is refused if it tries to guess. When the true date surfaces, a correction records it with its source.

Ghosted is a status, not a deletion

Silence is an outcome, and which companies go quiet is pattern knowledge. Terminal states stay on the books, one click away.

One open next action

Per pursuit, one thing to do next, with a due date. The Due page is your morning list; superseded intentions are kept, not erased.
