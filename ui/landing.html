Helpful is not the same as right

Issued means issued

An issued invoice never changes; a mistake is a void with a reason and a fresh number. Statements land whole or not at all, reconciled to the printed balances. Receipts must add up to their own lines.

Questions

Asked before people start

Anything else: hello@saybooks.io reaches the person who builds it.

Which one should I use, saybooks.io or the Mac app?

If you already use Claude and are fine with your books on a server, saybooks.io: nothing to install, add it as a connector and talk. If your books should never sit in someone else's cloud, the Mac app: the books, your documents and the model all stay on your Mac. Both keep the same books under the same rules.

Can I move my books from one to the other?

Yes, either way. Export your books as one file, then bring that file into empty books on the other side. The history and the audit trail come along.

Do I need a Claude subscription?

For saybooks.io, a Claude plan that allows connectors is the easiest way in; anything that speaks MCP works too, and the web app needs no AI at all. The Mac app needs no subscription: the model runs on the Mac.

Is it accounting software?

No. It keeps the operational record: invoices, receivables, orders, pipeline, reviewed spending. Your accountant's ledger stays where it is; Saybooks hands over balanced journal lines as a QuickBooks Online or Xero import file.

What does it cost?

Nothing today. saybooks.io is free and there is no paid plan yet; when there is, prices change with twelve months' notice. The Mac app is free while it is new and will become a paid app later. Export works on every version, so your books never depend on a price.

What happens to my books if Saybooks disappears?

Export takes everything, in JSON, audit log included, any day. The code is public under the AGPL and runs on a laptop.
