What the rules keep straight

The things a household's money gets wrong

Suggestions come to you to confirm. Nothing is quietly decided behind your back.

Counted once

Paying the card from checking shows on both statements. The two sides are paired, so the move is neither spending nor income, and a refund comes off the purchase it reverses instead of looking like income.

Loans that tie to the lender

Each payment is split into principal, interest and escrow, as the lender printed it or by the schedule, and it says which. Record the lender's balance and it is checked against ours, to the cent.

Your money, your decision

Approving a bill and putting a vendor on autopay are refused to any agent, whatever its role. Your agent records what happened; nothing is assumed paid until a statement shows it.

Totals, not advice

Tag what matters at tax time and the year sums it, names the receipts still missing, and gives interest paid and earned. What is deductible stays your accountant's call; there are no budgets telling you what to do.

Your data, actually yours

Nothing touches your bank

  • Files are the boundaryNo bank feed and no bank password, by design. Your agent reads the statements you hand it, and a statement is taken whole when its rows add up to its printed balances, or refused with the gap named.
  • It never moves moneySaybooks records what happened. It never pays a bill, charges a card or sends anything; you stay the only one who does.
  • Export and deleteOne click downloads every table as one file, audit log included. One button permanently removes your space, its database and every access key.
  • Or keep it on your MacThe same books run in Saybooks for Mac, with a model on your own machine. Move them across any time.

The rules it keeps are written down and executed: the personal finances spec and the loans spec. Also free: invoicing for freelancers and a job-hunt tracker.
